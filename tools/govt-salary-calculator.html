Salary Ledger

Basis: Basic Pay is read directly from the 7th CPC Pay Matrix (entry pay per level, +3% per cell, rounded to the nearest ₹100 — the Commission's own progression rule). HRA follows the current higher slab (X 30% / Y 20% / Z 10%) applicable since DA crossed 50%. Transport Allowance follows the 2017-revised rates by level and city, with DA-on-TA added at your DA rate. NPS deducts 10% of (Basic + DA) from pay; the matching 14% government contribution is not shown here as it isn't part of your in-hand salary. CGEGIS and CGHS are shown at commonly applicable slabs — confirm against your own pay slip, as local orders can vary.

This is an estimation tool, not a payslip. Actual recoveries (income tax, GPF/CPF loan installments, HBA/vehicle advance EMIs, etc.) are specific to each employee and department.
