Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep 2026 · Applies to ignitedtools.in and its subpages

iGNiTED Tools is a small, independently run collection of browser tools. This page explains what information is and isn't collected when you use it.

What the tools themselves do with your input

The calculators, overlays, and games on this site run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Numbers and text you type into a calculator — salary figures, temperature readings, and so on — are processed on your device and are never transmitted to, or stored on, any server we control.

Cookies and similar technology

The site itself does not set cookies to identify you or track your activity. However, third-party services used on the site — advertising and analytics — may set their own cookies or use similar technology, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use a web analytics service (such as Google Analytics) to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site — for example, which tools are used most, and which pages could use improvement. This data is anonymized or pseudonymized where the service allows, and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Information you send us directly

If you email us through the contact page, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that message (typically your email address and message content). This is used solely to respond to you and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Game leaderboards

Snake Royale has a global Top 10 leaderboard shared across everyone who plays. If you play and finish a run, the display name you typed and your score are sent to a hosted database (Google Firebase) so all visitors can see the same list. No other information — no email, no device data, no location — is collected through this feature. Don't use your real name if you'd rather stay anonymous; any name you enter is public on the leaderboard.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can use most browsers' settings to block or delete cookies at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through the Google and industry links above.
  • You can contact us to ask what information, if any, we hold about you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add a new analytics or advertising provider — we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
