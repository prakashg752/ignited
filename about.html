Why this site exists

One builder · no team · updated when something needs fixing

iGNiTED Tools is built and maintained by one person — me — PGP, a [Scientific Assitant in India Meteorological Department"] who kept running into the same problem: small, specific calculations that should take ten seconds were taking twenty minutes, because the tool that did them either didn't exist, cost money, or buried the answer under three pop-ups and a sign-up form.

The first few things I built were mostly born out of curiosity and everyday problems I wanted to solve. I wanted simple, reliable tools that gave me the information I was looking for without unnecessary complexity, so I started building them myself. Over time, those small experiments turned into a collection of useful calculators, utilities, and little web projects. Some were created to solve a specific problem, while others were simply a way to understand something better or experiment with a new idea. I also enjoy building things around my other interests, especially technology, content creation, and the web. Not everything starts with a grand plan — sometimes it is just a random idea that seems interesting enough to build. This website is where I keep those projects together. They are simple, practical, and occasionally a little experimental, but each one was built with the same goal: make something useful and share it with others who might find it helpful.

How tools get built and maintained

Everything on this site is something I use myself, or built for my own use first. That's a deliberate filter — it keeps the list short, but it also means every tool has at least one real user (me) checking it regularly. When something breaks or a number looks wrong, I hear about it because I'm usually the first person to notice.

I don't have a team, a release schedule, or a roadmap I can promise. New tools show up when I need one badly enough to build it, or when someone writes in with a request that matches that description. You can see what's actually changed, and when, on the changelog.

How it's funded

The site runs a small amount of display advertising to cover hosting costs and the time spent on upkeep. There's no subscription, no "pro" version, and nothing behind a paywall. If that ever changes for a specific tool, it'll say so clearly on that tool's page before you use it.

Have a tool idea, or found something that's calculating wrong? Get in touch — I read every message myself.
